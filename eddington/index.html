Eddington at a glance

Eddington is a census-designated place of about 0.55 square miles. It had 1,906 residents in 2010 and 1,996 in 2020. The Eddington station serves SEPTA's Trenton Line, and I-95 is close by.

A local guide places it between Route 13 and the river, with residential streets alongside industrial parks. Another guide calls it highly residential and notes the Bensalem Township Country Club nearby. Exact borders vary by map, so we use those roads as anchors.

What housing data says, and what it does not

NeighborhoodScout's profile for Eddington and Cornwells Heights reports that many homes were built between 1940 and 1969, with some from 1970 to 1999, mostly small to medium single-family houses and some apartment complexes, mostly owner occupied. That is a tract-level summary, not a survey of your block.

We do not assume that a house of that era has any particular pipe material or problem. If you want to know what you have, a plumber can check the visible pipes and, for the drain line, use a camera.

A simple plan for a slow or backed-up drain

  1. 1

    Find the pattern

    Note whether one fixture or several are slow. Several slow fixtures point to the main line.

  2. 2

    Stop adding water

    Hold off on laundry and long showers if drains are gurgling or backing up.

  3. 3

    Avoid harsh chemicals

    Strong drain chemicals can harm pipes and are unsafe for the person who works on the drain next.

  4. 4

    Call and describe it

    Tell the plumber where the cleanout is, if you know, and which drains are affected.
