Information from phone calls

This site does not use contact forms. The way to reach us is by phone. When you call, we may ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so a plumber can plan the visit.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information from the website

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and may show a map from Google Maps. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.

We do not ask you to create an account on this site.

How we use and share information

We use the details you give us to schedule and complete plumbing work, to answer your questions and to improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Questions about this policy can go to contact@expressplumberbensalem.com.

Where we dispatch from Bensalem

Our emergency line covers Bensalem Township and the nearby Lower Bucks communities of Croydon, Feasterville-Trevose, Levittown, Bristol and Langhorne.
