Emergency Water Service Line Repair in Bensalem, PA

Water service line repair in Bensalem fixes the pipe that carries water from the utility's connection to your house. It is buried, so a leak is easy to miss. The clues are a wet strip of lawn, a bill that climbed with no change in use, or low pressure throughout the house.

A leak between the meter and the house

A buried line that fails can wash out soil, flood a basement wall or leave you with no water. Call at any hour. If the leak is on your side of the meter, shutting the house main may slow it, and the meter shutoff may need the utility.

These steps help while you wait. New service line pipe and fittings must meet drinking water standards and International Plumbing Code (IPC) requirements, and the utility may set its own rules near the meter.

  • !Shut the main valve inside the house and check whether the leak slows.
  • !Check the meter reading with all fixtures off to confirm a leak.
  • !Keep people away from soft or sunken ground near the line.
  • !Note any wet patches, and take photos.

Who is responsible for which part

In general, the water utility owns the main in the street and the connection to it, and the homeowner owns the service line from that connection to the house, though this differs by utility and by address. Check your water bill or call your water utility to learn where the responsibility line sits at your property.

That detail decides who repairs what and who pays. Ask before work starts, and ask your utility whether it requires its own inspection or a specific process for repairs near the meter.

Trenchless and open-cut options

Some service lines can be replaced without trenching the entire run. A small entry pit at each end and a pull-through method lay new pipe with less lawn damage. Not every line suits it, and soil, depth and obstacles decide.

Open-cut is simpler and sometimes the only choice. A plumber explains both and tells you what each would do to your lawn, driveway or walk. For related leak tracing inside the house, see leak detection.
