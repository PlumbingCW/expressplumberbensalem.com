Nottingham within Bensalem

Bensalem Weekly's neighborhood guide lists Nottingham as central to the township and describes a large residential area of curved streets, manicured lawns and cul-de-sacs. Real estate sites also list Nottingham as a Bensalem neighborhood. Because sources describe the boundaries differently, this page does not draw a border.

NeighborhoodScout's profile says many homes in the tract were built between 1940 and 1969, with some from 1970 to 1999. It lists small to medium single-family homes plus apartment complexes, occupied by a mix of owners and renters.

If you rent, start here

Responsibility for plumbing repairs depends on your lease and the property owner.

  • ✓Read your lease for who handles repairs.
  • ✓Tell the landlord or property manager in writing, with a photo.
  • ✓Close a fixture shutoff if water is spilling, and tell the landlord where you closed it.
  • ✓Do not hire a plumber for rented space without the owner's approval.

If you own your home

For owners, the first steps are the same ones that help in any plumbing problem. Find the main shutoff, note the age of your water heater and learn which fixtures have their own shutoff valves.

A leaking toilet supply line, a worn flapper and a dripping faucet are among the most common household fixture problems, and they are usually quick to describe on the phone.
