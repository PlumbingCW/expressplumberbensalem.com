Plumbing in Bensalem: what makes it different

Bensalem is the southernmost township in Bucks County and the most populous municipality in the county, with about 62,700 residents across 21 square miles. It borders Northeast Philadelphia and the Delaware River, and Neshaminy Creek runs along its eastern edge. That mix of old and newer housing means no two plumbing calls look alike.

The practical result is that we ask questions first. How old is the house, where is the shutoff and what did the water do? Those answers decide the tools. The township's Building and Planning office at 215-633-3644 handles plumbing permits, and we tell you when a job needs one.
