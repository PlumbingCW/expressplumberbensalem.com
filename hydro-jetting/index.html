Emergency Hydro Jetting in Bensalem, PA

Hydro jetting in Bensalem uses a high-pressure water stream to scour the inside of a drain or sewer line. Where a snake pokes a hole through a clog, a jet washes the pipe wall clean. It is the standard answer when a line keeps clogging after repeated cabling.

The drain that clears and then clogs again

Homeowners often describe the same cycle. A plumber snakes the line, water drains, and a few weeks later the line is slow again. That pattern means the clog is the pipe lining, not a single wad. Grease, soap residue and mineral scale narrow the pipe a little more each week.

A jet scours the wall back to bare pipe. The cleaning head points rearward jets that pull the hose forward and flush debris out. When the line is clear, the pipe is nearly its full diameter again. That is why jetting tends to last longer than cabling alone.

Sewage backup and jetting

A main line that has backed up into the house is an emergency. A plumber may first clear it with a cable to restore flow, then recommend jetting once the flood risk is under control. The line is answered 24/7, and same-day service is offered when the schedule allows.

Why commercial kitchens and apartment lines jet regularly

Restaurants and multi-unit buildings put more grease and more users through a single line. Scheduled jetting is routine maintenance for them, and it costs far less than an emergency backup during a busy night. Property managers often set up annual plans.

The same logic applies to a large family home with a long line to the street. Ask about the commercial plumbing services we offer if you manage a business, and about sewer line cleaning for a residential schedule.

What a jetting visit looks like

The plumber locates the cleanout, feeds a flexible hose with a specially shaped nozzle into the line and starts the water. Different nozzles handle grease, scale, roots and sand. The hose is pulled back slowly so the debris travels to a point where it can be flushed to the main sewer.

A camera pass afterward confirms the result and shows any damage the cleaning revealed. If the camera finds a crack or an offset joint, the conversation turns to sewer line repair and the options for fixing it.
