Langhorne the borough and Langhorne the mailing address

Langhorne Borough has about 1,600 residents and was incorporated in 1876, when it took the name Langhorne in place of Attleborough. ZIP code 19047 covers the borough and most of neighboring Middletown Township, so a Langhorne address may be in the borough or in the township. Part of 19053 is also delivered by the Langhorne post office.

For a plumbing job, that difference decides who handles permits and inspections. A borough address and a township address have different offices. Check your tax bill for the municipality and call that office if a permit is needed.

We can help you sort it out. Whether the borough or the township issues the permit, plumbing work is held to International Plumbing Code (IPC) standards.

Finding the right township office from a Langhorne address

Because a Langhorne mailing address stretches beyond the borough, the first step for any permitted work is to confirm the municipality. Your tax bill or the township website names it. Then call that office to ask what the project needs.

A plumber can handle the work, but permits and inspections come from the local office. A contractor who asks which municipality you live in is doing the right thing, and so are you when you check before work begins. It also keeps a permit from landing at the wrong office.
