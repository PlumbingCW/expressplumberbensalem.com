When a clogged drain is an emergency

Wastewater coming up through a floor drain, a toilet that will not flush, or sewage in a tub means the main line is blocked. Do not wait for morning. Standing wastewater damages flooring quickly and carries bacteria into the living space.

Call the 24/7 line and keep these steps in mind while a plumber is on the way. Same-day and after-hours visits are available when the schedule allows, and the call is answered at any hour. Safety steps matter as much as tools: what to do first is stop the water, and do not mix chemical cleaners. Drain repairs afterward follow International Plumbing Code (IPC) standards.

  • !Stop running water everywhere in the house, including the washing machine and dishwasher.
  • !Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a backed-up line. It sits in the pipe and burns skin when a plumber opens the drain.
  • !Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the lowest floor drain or tub.
  • !Note which fixtures are affected. A plumber can place the clog from that list alone.

What your drains are telling you

Where the symptom shows up points to where the blockage lives. These patterns come up again and again on service calls.

  • ✓One sink drains slowly while every other fixture is fine: the clog is in that fixture's own trap or branch line.
  • ✓A toilet bubbles when the shower drains: the vent or the line they share is partly blocked.
  • ✓The lowest drain in the house gurgles or overflows when upstairs fixtures run: the main line is blocked below that point.
  • ✓Drains clear for a few weeks, then slow again: grease, scale or roots are narrowing the pipe instead of fully blocking it.
  • ✓A foul smell from a floor drain with no visible water: a dry trap or a partial blockage, and worth a look before it becomes a backup.

Kitchen sinks, tubs and the washing machine standpipe

Kitchen sinks fail more often than any other fixture because cooking fat cools inside the pipe and hardens. A hand auger breaks the plug, but the greasy lining stays behind and catches the next meal. That is why the same sink clogs again a month later.

Tubs and showers clog with hair wrapped around the drain stopper or the pop-up linkage. Pulling the stopper and clearing the first foot of pipe solves most of them. A washing machine standpipe is the third common culprit. Lint builds up, the standpipe overflows, and the laundry room floor floods mid-cycle.

If a drain keeps returning to slow, ask for a camera look instead of a third snaking. A sewer camera inspection shows whether the pipe is scaled, offset or cracked, and that changes the repair from cleaning to fixing.

Snaking, cabling and jetting: what each one really does

A hand snake works for the first few feet of a sink or tub line. A cable machine feeds a spinning steel cable through longer runs and cuts through roots and dense blockages. Both punch a hole through the clog, and the water drains again.

Jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe wall. It is the better fix for grease and scale that keep coming back, and it is covered on our hydro jetting page. If the line is cracked, none of these tools repair it. At that point the conversation moves to sewer line repair.

Bensalem homes and the main line

What affects the price of drain cleaning

The price is set by the job, not by a flat template. Five things move it.

Location of the clogA sink trap is quick. A main line behind a cleanout in the basement or yard takes more time and equipment.
Length of pipeA longer run needs more cable and more time to clear fully.
Clog materialRoots and scale call for cutting heads or a jet instead of a plain auger.
AccessA cleanout makes the job simpler. Without one, a plumber may have to pull a toilet.
Time of callAfter-hours dispatch costs more to staff than a scheduled daytime visit.

A note on insurance. Standard homeowners policies usually cover sudden water damage but often treat sewer or drain backup as an optional add-on, and a gradual clog is a maintenance item. Check your policy language and ask the agent whether a backup endorsement is in place.
