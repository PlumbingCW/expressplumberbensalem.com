Sewer Camera Inspection in Bensalem, PA

A sewer camera inspection in Bensalem sends a small waterproof camera through your drain line and shows what is inside the pipe. It replaces guessing with video. You see roots, cracks, offsets, scale and low spots, and you decide what to do with real information.

If you are buying an older house, planning a repair or tired of the same drain clogging, an inspection is the cheapest way to learn the truth. We serve Bensalem, Cornwells Heights, Eddington, Andalusia and Trevose with a clear price before work starts.

What the camera actually shows

The camera head has lights and a cable marked in feet. As it travels, the plumber watches a live feed and notes distances to any defect. A locator on the surface can mark the spot above ground so a repair can be planned without a search.

The pipe wall tells a story. Scale and rough texture point to age and buildup. Hairline cracks and offset joints point to movement. A pooling section called a belly shows where the pipe has settled.

Roots show as hair-like masses at joints. Inspection findings are measured against the International Plumbing Code (IPC) standards for drain slope and joints, which is why a recorded pass is useful for repair planning.

How an inspection visit runs

A typical visit follows four steps.

  1. 1

    Find access

    A cleanout is best. If none exists, the camera may enter through a toilet flange or vent.

  2. 2

    Clear the way

    If the line is blocked, a plumber clears it first so the camera can travel.

  3. 3

    Record the pass

    The camera runs to the street connection while a recording is made.

  4. 4

    Review together

    You see the footage, hear the findings and get options for any defects.

Before you buy an older Bensalem home

Sewer laterals are out of sight, and sellers rarely know their condition. A camera pass during the inspection period gives the buyer facts to negotiate on. A sound line is a green light. A failing line is a known cost that can be priced into the deal.

Ask the inspector to run the camera all the way to the street connection and to give you the recording. If the footage shows damage, get a repair quote before you waive any contingency. Neighbors in Levittown and Croydon often face the same question when buying homes of similar age.

When a camera inspection is the right first step

Consider one if any of these apply.

  • ✓A drain has backed up more than once in a year.
  • ✓A cable stopped at the same point twice.
  • ✓You are buying or selling an older house.
  • ✓A patch of lawn above the line is sunken or soggy.
  • ✓You are planning a basement bathroom or remodel that adds load to the line.

Permits and records

An inspection itself does not usually need a permit, but any repair that follows may. Bensalem Township's Building and Planning office at 215-633-3644 can tell you what applies to repairs at your address.

Keep the recording. If you later file an insurance claim or sell the house, dated footage is useful evidence of the pipe's condition.
