Do this first

Water pouring from a pipe does not slow down by itself. The main shutoff valve stops the supply to the whole house, and every household member should know where it is. It is usually near the water meter, in the basement or a utility area.

After that, reduce the damage and call. Same-day service is offered when the schedule allows, and the line is answered at any hour. A repair that replaces a section of supply pipe is normally done to the International Plumbing Code (IPC) standards the township enforces, which is one reason a patch with the wrong fitting is not a real fix.

  • !Close the main shutoff valve, turning it clockwise until it stops.
  • !Open the lowest faucet and the highest faucet to drain the lines.
  • !Switch off power to wet areas if water is near outlets or the panel.
  • !Move belongings out of the way and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Why pipes burst

The most common cause in Pennsylvania is freezing. Water expands as it turns to ice, and the pressure splits the pipe, often at a spot away from where the ice formed. The pipe may not leak until it thaws, so the flood can start after the cold snap ends.

Other causes include old corroded pipe, high water pressure, a bad fitting and damage during remodel work. A plumber finds the cause before repairing it. Fixing the split without finding the reason only invites the next failure.

Preventing frozen pipes in winter

A few habits cut the risk before the next cold snap.

  • ✓Disconnect garden hoses and shut interior valves to outdoor spigots.
  • ✓Insulate pipes in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls.
  • ✓Open cabinet doors on exterior walls during extreme cold.
  • ✓Let a faucet drip when the forecast is far below freezing.
  • ✓Keep the thermostat set, even if you travel.

Water damage after a burst

Drying begins as soon as the water stops. Wet drywall, insulation and carpet can grow mold within days if left wet. A plumber repairs the pipe, and a water mitigation company dries the structure. Your insurer may have preferred vendors for the second step.

Keep receipts and photos. Insurers typically ask for them. If the burst was in a heating pipe, see boiler repair, since a failed heating loop has its own safety steps.
