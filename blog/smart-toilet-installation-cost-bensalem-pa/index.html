Smart Toilet Installation Cost: A Bensalem, PA Homeowner Guide

By the Bensalem Express Plumbing team. Published November 14, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

A smart toilet needs three things a standard toilet does not: a nearby power outlet, a water supply that suits its features and a sound floor flange. The installation cost depends mostly on whether the bathroom already has them. Swapping a standard toilet for a smart model is a simple job when the outlet is in place and a complicated one when it is not.

This guide explains what a smart toilet installation involves and what moves the price. We do not quote dollar figures here, because the right number depends on the bathroom.

Smart toilet or smart seat

Two different products get called by the same name.

OptionWhat it isInstall notes
Bidet seatReplaces the seat on a standard toiletNeeds an outlet and a T-fitting on the supply line
Integrated smart toiletToilet and bidet in one unitNeeds an outlet, a supply connection and often a specific rough-in
Tankless smart toiletNo tank, uses water line pressureNeeds sufficient water pressure and a larger supply line

What drives the installation price

Several factors set the scope.

  • ✓Whether a grounded outlet is already within reach of the toilet.
  • ✓The condition of the shutoff valve, which may need replacing.
  • ✓The size and type of the toilet's rough-in, 10, 12 or 14 inches.
  • ✓The condition of the floor flange and the subfloor.
  • ✓Disposal of the old toilet and the toilet model you chose.

Electrical work is the hidden step

Plumbers install the water side. A grounded outlet on a ground-fault protected circuit is an electrical job, and many bathrooms do not have one beside the toilet. Running one means an electrician, and that is a separate cost to plan for.

Ask your electrician or plumber to confirm the outlet type the product requires. Read the manual before buying, since some models need a dedicated circuit. Doing this before the toilet arrives avoids a second visit.

Water supply and pressure

Tankless smart toilets depend on water pressure and flow. If pressure is low or the supply line is small, the unit may flush weakly. A plumber can test pressure and tell you whether the home supports the model you have chosen.

If the shutoff valve is old, replace it during the install. A valve that has not moved in years can break when turned. Related fixture work is covered on our toilet repair page.

Local note: permits and who to ask

A like-for-like toilet swap generally does not need a permit, but electrical work and any change to drain lines may. Bensalem Township's Building and Planning office at 215-633-3644 can tell you what applies at your address.

If you are remodeling a bathroom, see plumbing installation. Homeowners in Levittown and Langhorne should confirm which municipal office covers their street before work starts.

How the install goes

A careful install follows four steps.

  1. 1

    Confirm the rough-in

    Measure from the wall to the center of the drain to match the model.

  2. 2

    Check outlet and supply

    Confirm the outlet, the shutoff valve and the supply line.

  3. 3

    Set the toilet

    Remove the old toilet, set a new wax ring or seal and mount the new unit.

  4. 4

    Connect and test

    Connect water, power and test every function and the seal.

Questions to settle before you buy a smart toilet

Measure first. The rough-in distance from the wall to the drain center, the space around the toilet and the height of the outlet all matter. A model that does not fit the room can cost more to fit than to return.

Read the product's requirements for water pressure and electrical supply. Many manuals list the minimum pressure and the type of outlet. Matching the product to the bathroom at the start saves a second visit.

Features to weigh against the install

Each feature adds a requirement.

  • ✓Heated seats and warm water need power.
  • ✓Automatic lids and sensors need power and sometimes batteries.
  • ✓A built-in tank may need extra clearance behind the toilet.
  • ✓A tankless design needs steady water pressure.
  • ✓Remote controls and apps add convenience and one more thing to maintain.

Maintenance after installation

Smart toilets have filters, nozzles and seals that need cleaning. Read the manual for the schedule and the approved cleaners. Harsh chemicals can damage the seals and the nozzle.

Keep the manual and the model number. If a part fails, the warranty and the part lookup both depend on them.
