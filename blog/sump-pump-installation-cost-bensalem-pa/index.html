Average Cost of Sump Pump Installation: A Bensalem, PA Price Guide

By the Bensalem Express Plumbing team. Published November 14, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

The cost of a sump pump installation depends on whether the pit already exists, which pump you choose and how far the water has to travel. Replacing a pump in an existing pit is a small job. Cutting a new pit in a concrete floor and adding a backup system is a larger one. A single average hides that range.

This guide explains what goes into the price so you can read a quote. We do not list dollar figures, because the right number comes from seeing the basement.

What changes the price

Five items drive most quotes.

ItemLower effortHigher effort
PitExisting pit in good shapeNew pit cut into the slab
Pump typeBasic submersibleHigher capacity or a pedestal replacement
BackupNoneBattery or water-powered backup
Discharge lineShort run outdoorsLong run, buried or tied into drainage
ElectricalOutlet nearbyNew circuit or a GFCI outlet

Replacement versus new installation

If you already have a pit and a discharge line, a replacement takes a short visit. The plumber removes the old pump, checks the pit, installs the new unit with a check valve and tests it. A pit that is cracked, undersized or full of silt may need work as well.

A new installation starts with cutting the slab, digging the pit and setting a liner. It is more labor and more mess. If you are finishing a basement or fixing a recurring seepage problem, see basement waterproofing for how the pump fits into a system.

Add-ons worth discussing

Ask about these when you get a quote.

  • ✓A battery backup pump for power outages during storms.
  • ✓A high-water alarm so you know when the pit is filling.
  • ✓A sealed pit cover to limit odor and humidity.
  • ✓A check valve to prevent water from flowing back into the pit.
  • ✓A discharge line that carries water well away from the foundation.

Local note: storms and groundwater

Bensalem sits between Neshaminy Creek and Poquessing Creek. When the remnants of Hurricane Ida passed through on September 1, 2021, Bucks County received 4 to 10 inches of rain and county officials reported Neshaminy Creek at 20.84 feet. That is a reminder that heavy rain can raise groundwater quickly.

A pump should be tested before the wet season. Homes in Croydon and Levittown near creek corridors may see the effects first. For service and repair, read our sump pump page.

Insurance and what it may not cover

Many standard homeowners policies exclude groundwater and sump pump failure unless an endorsement is added. That can make a pump a bigger financial question than it looks. Check your policy and ask your agent about water backup coverage.

A pump that fails during a storm without a backup is the typical loss. The cost of a backup system is often small compared with a flooded finished basement. Ask your plumber what they would recommend for your space.

Permits and contractor checks

A pump replacement may not need a permit, but a new pit, a new discharge line or electrical work might. Bensalem Township's Building and Planning office at 215-633-3644 can tell you what applies at your address.

Pennsylvania requires contractors who do $5,000 or more of home improvement work in a year to register with the Attorney General. You can verify at 1-888-520-6680. Ask for a written scope and a clear price before work starts.

How a plumber sizes a sump pump

Pump size depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it must be lifted. A pump that is too small runs constantly and wears out. A pump that is too large cycles on and off quickly and wears out as well.

A plumber looks at the pit size, the discharge height and the amount of water you see in storms. That tells them which pump and which backup fit your basement.

Maintenance that protects the investment

A pump that is checked lasts longer.

  • ✓Test it each season with a bucket of water.
  • ✓Clear silt and debris from the pit.
  • ✓Check the discharge outlet for ice, leaves or blockage.
  • ✓Replace the battery in a backup system on its schedule.
  • ✓Keep the manual and the installation date.
