Slow Draining Sink That Is Not Clogged: A Bensalem, PA Guide

By the Bensalem Express Plumbing team. Published November 14, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

A sink that drains slowly but does not seem clogged usually has a partial blockage further down the line, a vent problem or buildup coating the pipe wall. You cleared the trap and the stopper, and the water still crawls. That is a sign the problem is past what you can reach.

This guide explains the usual causes, a few simple tests and when to call a plumber. It also covers why a plunger sometimes makes no difference.

Simple tests to narrow the cause

A few minutes of testing helps a plumber help you.

  1. 1

    Test other fixtures

    Run the tub, the toilet and another sink. If they are slow too, the main line is likely involved.

  2. 2

    Listen for gurgles

    Gurgling points toward venting or a partial blockage.

  3. 3

    Check the disposal

    Run water with the disposal off. Slow drainage with the disposal on or off narrows the suspect.

  4. 4

    Fill and release

    Fill the sink and watch how the water drains. A steady slow draw suggests buildup. A glug suggests venting.

Why grease and soap build up quietly

A kitchen pipe does not clog all at once. Grease cools and sticks to the wall, food particles stick to the grease and soap residue adds more. The channel shrinks a little each week. Water still passes, only slower.

A plunger does little against that coating. It pushes water against a pipe that is mostly open. A cable punches a hole through the buildup, and a jet scours it away.

For a repeat problem, a camera shows whether the wall is coated or damaged. Drain slope and venting follow International Plumbing Code (IPC) standards, which is why a sag or a bad vent can cause slow drainage.

What to avoid

These habits make slow drains worse.

  • ✓Pouring cooking grease or oil down the drain.
  • ✓Using chemical cleaners repeatedly, which can damage older pipes.
  • ✓Putting coffee grounds, rice or pasta in the disposal.
  • ✓Ignoring early gurgling, which signals airflow problems.
  • ✓Waiting until the sink fully backs up before calling.

Local note: older lines and where to ask

Bensalem's housing includes many ages, and older drain lines scale up faster than newer ones. We do not claim a specific pipe type for any neighborhood. A camera inspection shows what is in your line, as described on our sewer inspection page.

If several drains are slow and your neighbors report the same, the township's main may be involved. Bensalem Township's main number is 215-633-3600. Homeowners in Levittown and Bristol may reach different municipal offices, so ask the one that covers your address.

When to call a plumber

Call if more than one fixture is slow, if the sink gurgles or if the problem returns after clearing. A plumber can tell whether it is a branch, the vent or the main line, and can bring the right equipment. Quick calls are cheaper than a backed-up main line.

If the slow drain is in the kitchen, the answer is often a cable plus a jet. Our drain cleaning page explains the options in more detail.

A simple routine to keep sinks draining

Once a week, run hot water for a minute after washing dishes. It does not clear every film, but it keeps grease moving. Use a drain strainer to catch food, and wipe greasy pans before washing.

In the bathroom, a hair catcher costs little and prevents most clogs. Pull the stopper every few months and clean the first few inches of the drain.

When a slow drain is a warning

These signs say the problem is bigger than the sink.

  • ✓Several fixtures drain slowly at the same time.
  • ✓A toilet gurgles when the sink drains.
  • ✓Water backs up in a floor drain or a tub.
  • ✓A sewer smell appears near drains or in the yard.
  • ✓The slow drain returns within weeks of clearing.
