How to Waterproof a Basement Floor: A Bensalem, PA Homeowner Guide

By the Bensalem Express Plumbing team. Published November 14, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

Waterproofing a basement floor starts by finding where the water comes from. Coatings and sealers help with dampness, but they do not stop water that is pushing up through a crack or the joint at the wall. A drain and a sump pump handle that, and grading and gutters handle the rain before it arrives.

This guide explains the common sources of floor water, which fixes fit each one and when a plumber should be involved.

Where the water comes from

The pattern on the floor tells you a lot.

What you seeLikely sourceFix to consider
Wet edge where the floor meets the wallGroundwater at the jointInterior drain and sump pump
Puddle in the middle after rainCrack in the slabCrack repair, drainage
Damp but no standing waterMoisture vapor through the slabSealer or coating, dehumidifier
Water near the laundry or heaterPlumbing leakFind and fix the leak, see [[leak-detectionleak detection]]
Water after every stormPoor grading or short downspoutsFix gutters and grade first

A sensible order of operations

Fixing the cheapest cause first avoids paying for the wrong system.

  1. 1

    Fix the outside first

    Extend downspouts away from the house and make sure the ground slopes away from the foundation.

  2. 2

    Test for moisture

    Tape a square of plastic to the floor for a day. Moisture under it means vapor. Moisture on top means condensation.

  3. 3

    Rule out plumbing

    Check pipes, the water heater and the washer for leaks.

  4. 4

    Choose the system

    If water enters as liquid, an interior drain and a pump are the standard answer.

What floor coatings can and cannot do

A waterproofing paint or sealer slows moisture vapor that passes through concrete. It can help with a damp smell and a chalky floor. It will not hold back water pressing from underneath, and it can peel when pressure builds behind it.

If you see liquid water, treat the cause instead of the surface. Putting a coating over an active leak hides it for a season and then fails. An honest assessment looks at the source first.

What an interior drain system includes

The usual setup has these parts.

  • ✓A channel along the floor edge that collects water.
  • ✓A sump pit that receives the water.
  • ✓A sump pump, ideally with a battery backup.
  • ✓A discharge line that carries water away from the house.
  • ✓A sealed cover to control humidity and odor.

Local note: creeks, storms and groundwater

Bensalem sits between Poquessing Creek on the west and Neshaminy Creek on the east, and both are part of the township's setting. During the remnants of Hurricane Ida on September 1, 2021, Bucks County saw 4 to 10 inches of rain, and county officials reported Neshaminy Creek cresting at 20.84 feet. We do not claim any specific basement flooded then.

The practical point is simple. Heavy rain raises groundwater, and a basement that is borderline in normal weather fails in a big storm.

Test your pump now. Homeowners in Croydon and Levittown near creeks should pay extra attention. Read the sump pump page for the pump side.

Waterproofing and finishing a basement

If you plan to finish the space, treat water control as step one. Carpet, drywall and furniture hold moisture and grow mold. A dry floor, a working pump and a dehumidifier make the finished space last.

Mention water history to any contractor, including past floods and where they appeared. The better they understand it, the better the plan. A professional assessment is worth it before you spend on finishes, as covered in basement waterproofing.

A seasonal checklist for basement water

Spring and fall are good times to walk the property. Clean gutters, check that downspouts discharge away from the foundation and look for soil that has settled against the wall. Fixing these costs very little compared with interior work.

Inside, test the sump pump and look at the floor edge after the first heavy rain. A thin line of dampness at the wall base is an early warning that groundwater is rising.

What to keep off a wet basement floor

A few habits protect belongings while you sort out the cause.

  • ✓Cardboard boxes, which wick moisture and collapse.
  • ✓Wood furniture without feet or a pallet under it.
  • ✓Electronics and documents, which belong on shelves.
  • ✓Carpets or rugs that trap moisture against the slab.
  • ✓Chemicals that could leak and mix if water enters.
