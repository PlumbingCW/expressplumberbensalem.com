How to tell when replacement beats repair

A tank that leaks from its body cannot be patched. Water has escaped through the steel shell, and the same corrosion that caused one leak is advancing elsewhere. That single symptom settles the question.

Without a leak, the decision comes from age and repair history. Most storage tanks are designed for roughly a decade of service. When a heater is in that range and needs a major part, a new unit usually makes better use of the same money. The water heater repair page explains the cases where fixing is smarter.

A failed tank at night

A tank failure is time-sensitive because the water keeps flowing until the supply is shut off. Do the shutoff steps first, then call. The line is answered 24/7, and a plumber can often be scheduled for the same day if the schedule allows.

While waiting, protect what you can. A wet vac handles small amounts, and moving stored items off the floor limits losses.

  • !Close the cold-water supply valve at the heater.
  • !Switch off the breaker for electric heaters or set the gas control to off.
  • !Open a hot faucet on the highest floor so the tank drains instead of pushing water out of the leak.
  • !Take photos of the unit, the rating plate and the damage for your insurer.

What a replacement visit includes

Scope varies by home, but a careful swap covers these steps.

  • ✓Drain and disconnect the old unit, and remove it from the house.
  • ✓Check the shutoff valves, supply lines and venting for age and fit.
  • ✓Install a new unit with an expansion tank where the system calls for one.
  • ✓Run the relief valve discharge pipe to a safe point.
  • ✓Fill, purge air, restore power or gas and test for leaks.
  • ✓Explain the temperature setting and where each valve lives.

Permits, codes and who to ask

Bensalem Township has opted into Pennsylvania's Uniform Construction Code and uses the International Plumbing Code. Permit rules for water heater swaps vary, so ask the township's Building and Planning office at 215-633-3644 what applies at your address. The office is at 2400 Byberry Road and posts hours of 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday through Friday.

A reputable installer tells you what the township requires before the job starts. In Pennsylvania, contractors who do $5,000 or more of home improvement work in a year must be registered with the Attorney General, and the toll-free verification line is 1-888-520-6680. Check it before you sign anything.

Sizing for the household

A bigger tank is not automatically better. A two-person household with a 40-gallon tank is fine, while a family with teenagers and a soaking tub needs more recovery or a larger unit. The first-hour rating on the unit's yellow label tells you how much hot water it can deliver in a busy hour.

In neighborhoods such as Levittown, where the houses are compact, a short vent run and a closet install often dictate the shape of the choice. Size, venting and clearance are settled on site, not from a catalog.
