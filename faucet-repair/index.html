Signs a faucet or valve needs attention

Fixtures usually announce trouble before they fail.

  • ✓A steady drip from the spout after the handle is off.
  • ✓Water leaking at the base of the faucet or under the sink.
  • ✓A handle that is stiff, loose or spins without control.
  • ✓Low pressure at one fixture while the others are fine.
  • ✓A shutoff valve that will not turn or weeps at the stem.

What to do when water will not stop at a fixture

A faucet that will not shut off or a supply line that has burst under a sink needs the shutoff valve closed immediately. If the valve is seized or does not exist, use the main shutoff.

Call at any hour. If the main valve does not stop the water, treat it as an emergency. Replacement faucets and valves should meet the plumbing standards the IPC points to, and the maker's installation guidelines decide the supply line size.

  • !Close the shutoff valve under the sink, or the main valve if that fails.
  • !Open the faucet to drain the line and reduce pressure.
  • !Dry the cabinet and floor to prevent mold and swelling.
  • !Take a photo of the damage if a leak soaked the cabinet.

Repair or replace the fixture

A cartridge or washer repair is quick and low cost when the faucet body is sound. If the finish is pitted, the body is corroded or parts are no longer available, replacing the faucet makes more sense than patching it.

A replacement is also the time to check the shutoff valves under the sink. Old valves that have not been turned in years often seize or leak when touched. Replacing them while the sink is open costs little and prevents a later emergency.

Outdoor spigots and winter

Pennsylvania winters freeze unprotected outdoor faucets. A hose left attached can trap water in the spigot, and when it freezes the pipe inside the wall can split. The damage often goes unseen until spring, when the faucet is turned on and water floods the wall.

Disconnect hoses before the first freeze and shut off interior valves feeding outdoor spigots if your home has them. If a spigot drips inside the wall in spring, call right away, and see burst pipe repair if water is already escaping.
