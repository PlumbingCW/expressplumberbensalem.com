No hot water or water on the floor

A heater that has stopped heating is an inconvenience. A heater that is leaking is an emergency, because a tank failure can release dozens of gallons in minutes. Cold-water shutoff and power or gas shutoff are the first moves.

Call the emergency line at any hour. Same-day service is offered when the schedule allows, and the steps below help limit damage until a plumber arrives. Repairs that touch gas valves, relief valves or venting should follow the maker's guidelines and International Plumbing Code (IPC) standards.

  • !Shut the cold-water supply valve on top of the heater and open one hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure.
  • !Turn an electric unit off at the breaker. For gas, set the control to pilot or off and do not relight it.
  • !Water running from the relief valve discharge pipe is not the same as a leaking tank, so note exactly where the water appears.
  • !Smell gas or hear a hiss near the heater: leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.

Common failures and what they usually mean

Technicians sort water heater problems by symptom first. This table shows how the sort works and where repair stops making sense.

Lukewarm water, electric unitFailed heating element or thermostatUsually repair

Questions that decide repair versus replacement

Four facts settle most decisions. Write them down before the technician arrives.

  • ✓Age of the unit. The date is on the rating plate, and a tank in its second decade has less repair value.
  • ✓Where the leak is. A leaking fitting or valve is a repair. A leaking tank body is not.
  • ✓How often it has needed service. Two repairs in two years usually costs more than a new heater.
  • ✓Whether the household has grown. A heater that cannot keep up is a sizing problem repair cannot fix.

Leaks from the heater, from above it and from the floor beside it

Not every wet spot near a heater comes from the heater. Condensation on cold supply pipes, a dripping relief valve line and a leaking washing machine hose can all end up on the same floor. A technician traces the water to its source before recommending parts.

If the leak is at the base of the tank, plan for replacement and read about water heater replacement so the discussion starts informed. If the water comes from a corroded fitting on top, the repair is small and the tank may have years left.

Tankless units, flushing and error codes

Tankless heaters rarely fail all at once. They throw an error code when scale narrows the heat exchanger, when a vent is blocked or when the flow is too low to fire the burner. Many calls end with a descaling flush rather than a part.

The detail on service intervals and flush procedures sits on the tankless water heater page. Tell the dispatcher the error code on the display. It speeds up the diagnosis.

Why Bensalem households tend to call about this

Most Bensalem homes rely on a single storage tank heater. When that tank sits in a finished basement or a utility closet near living space, a slow leak can run for days before anyone notices.

A drain pan with a pipe to a floor drain or a water alarm costs little and buys time. If the heater sits in a basement that has taken water before, see how sump pump installation and repair fits into protecting the equipment itself.

What shapes a water heater repair bill

Repair prices vary by part and by access. These five points are the biggest drivers.

Fuel typeGas valves and burner parts cost differently than heating elements and thermostats.
Unit ageParts for older models may take longer to find.
LocationA heater in a tight attic or crawl space takes longer to service than a basement unit.
Code itemsMissing expansion tank or discharge pipe fixes add to the work.
Time of callAfter-hours dispatch carries a different labor rate than a booked daytime visit.

Insurance note. Sudden tank failure that damages a floor is often covered by a homeowners policy, while the heater itself usually is not. Keep photos and the failed part if possible.
